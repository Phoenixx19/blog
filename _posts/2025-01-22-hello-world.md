### Reasons behind the blog
So why did I need to start a blog? I think there are multiple reasons about it when I come to think about it.

1. **Inspiration**: [.Gotch(ごっち) ’s blog](https://blog.yougoto.dev/){:target="_blank"} really inspired me to start one. His blog feels almost like a diary, and I really resonated on that idea. A safe place where you post your doings, your thoughts, your.. anything, without any stakes involved, it’s just you (and your anonymous readers/admirers, if there are any).

2. **Social media detox**: I started using Twitter a while ago to share my opinions, thoughts and projects there but also for the funny animals and memes that the platform had. But slowly, the platform grew to become more and more toxic and after the takeover of a certain person (which shall remain nameless), I decided to search for alternatives. Even though I did switch to another social media down the line, I never actually felt comfortable starting posting there.

3. **Getting it out**: Not sure if it’s just my personal baggage, but it’s really hard to share, vent out frustrations or just dump all my passions onto something/someone. It doesn’t help that I never had the will to start a diary. But since my departure from Twitter I had the urge to share again something, ANYTHING!

So basically, killing three birds with one stone!

### Themes for this blog

### “Scheduling"
Since I want to have fun while blogging, I don’t want to impose myself with a weekly, biweekly or monthly limit to get a post released but I would like to release at least one post monthly even though I do this based on my free time... sooo yeah. 
I don’t want you to make you wait for it either. So, if you are interested in any future posts, I’ll recommend you to join the [**RSS feed**]({% link feed.xml %}) (using whatever RSS client, if you have any) so that you don’t miss the next post when it will be released.
